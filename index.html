About

01

I am a Chemical Engineering MASc student at the University of Toronto and a graduate researcher in Prof. Benjamin Sanchez-Lengeling’s Chemical Cognition Lab at the Vector Institute.

I build deep probabilistic models for molecules and use them to guide experiments under uncertainty, from generative models of protein subpockets to Gaussian processes for low-data polymer property prediction.

I spent two years as a synthetic and analytical chemist before moving to machine learning, and that bench experience shapes both my modelling and my teaching.

Outside the lab I race for the Canadian National Dragon Boat Team (3× gold, 3× silver at the 2025 IDBF World Championships) and the Iron Dragons Paddling Club, and I was President of the U of T Run Club from 2024 to 2025, leading the campus’ largest student organization.
